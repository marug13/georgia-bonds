❓ Addressing Key Concerns

Is this risky or experimental?

No - it's proven technology

Tokenized bonds use the same legal structure as traditional bonds, just with blockchain settlement. Multiple jurisdictions have successfully issued them (Singapore, Switzerland, World Bank). You maintain full regulatory control.

Will this destabilize our currency?

No - it strengthens the GEL

Foreign investors still need to purchase GEL to buy GEL-denominated bonds. This actually increases demand for GEL. You maintain all existing capital controls.

Is Solana credible enough?

Yes - institutional grade

Solana is a top-5 blockchain globally, used by Visa, Shopify, and explored by various central banks. It processes 3,000+ transactions per second with 99.9%+ uptime. The Solana Foundation offers full technical support.

What about regulatory compliance?

You stay in full control

This is just a new distribution channel - like adding online banking to physical branches. All existing securities laws, KYC/AML requirements, and investor protections remain in place. You set all the rules.

What if blockchain technology fails?

Built-in redundancy

Traditional bonds remain valid and enforceable regardless of technology. Blockchain is just the settlement layer - the legal obligation remains unchanged. You're not dependent on any single technology.

Will this be expensive to implement?

Cost-effective solution

Solana Foundation is offering technical infrastructure. Implementation costs are minimal compared to potential savings from lower borrowing costs. Many administrative costs actually decrease with automation.
